# AWE Faith hoodie designs

Prepared 15 October 2026. These are original layouts; no GLO artwork or source pixels were copied.

Both designs include a freshly drawn A.W.E. Missions ring-and-cross logo on the front, their main wording across the back and repeated down BOTH sleeves, Ephesians 2:8–9 in KJV underneath the back heading, then a black-on-white QR code and subtle AWE Faith branding. The second design replaces FORGIVEN with ACTION / WORSHIP / ENCOURAGE. Its verse and QR stay in the same locations.

The QR links directly to https://awemissions.github.io/Awe-faith/ — the existing app's Share URL. The live destination returned HTTP 200. The standalone QR, both back plates, and the browser-exported back PNG were independently decoded. Always scan a physical sample before selling: folds, print quality and fabric affect scanning.

## Open and edit

Start the updated Merch Studio, select a prepared design and click **Open prepared design**. Save the current project first if you want to keep it. You can also use **Open project** with either `.awefaith.json` file in this folder's design subfolders.

The Front, Back, Left sleeve and Right sleeve tabs each support image/text placement and transparent PNG export. **All print areas** exports each area separately. Saved projects retain all four areas; older front/back projects still open. These initial layouts use one embedded print plate per area; replace that area's image to independently redraw the logo, verse or QR. They are not separate editable text objects.

Planning front/back files are 3600 × 4800 px (12 × 16 in at 300 DPI). Sleeve files are 1200 × 3600 px (4 × 12 in). These are planning canvases, NOT confirmed Printful garment templates. Logo is approximately 3.5 in wide; back heading 11 in wide; QR including its four-module white quiet zone is 2.7 in wide on the planning canvas. Keep the QR black on white, including the quiet zone. The app blocks draft creation if preset scaling would make the QR smaller than 2 inches or the sleeve template is shorter than 8 inches.

## Garment and placement decision

- Preferred oversized fit: **AS Colour 5161 / Unisex Relax Hoodie**. Printful lists separate back, left/right sleeve DTG placements and separate long left/right sleeve DTFlex placements. Its public color list does not include charcoal. Do not relabel Black or Athletic Heather as charcoal.
- Charcoal alternative: **Lane Seven LS14001**, listed in Charcoal Heather, with separate back and left/right sleeve DTG placements. This is a regular-fit hoodie; Printful suggests sizing up for a looser fit. Do not market it as a true oversized blank.
- Keep charcoal as a concept until the selected account's live catalog verifies a blank, exact color, sizes and available technique. The current automation uses the v1 catalog print templates; it does not select DTFlex techniques. If the desired long-sleeve placement is only available through DTFlex or missing in that API, use Printful Design Maker to finish those placements manually.
- A single continuous print across the back and shoulder/sleeve seams is not configured. These layouts repeat complete wording in separate areas, avoiding an alignment promise. All-over cut-and-sew would be a separate product and template setup.
