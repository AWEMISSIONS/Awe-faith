Strength Under Christ

Biblical strength is not control or self-sufficiency. It is strength submitted to Christ and used to serve others.

ESV
Central passage · Mark 10:35–45 · Read full passage in ESV ↗“Whoever would be great among you must be your servant.”

Read the full passage in the ESV, then read the surrounding chapter for context. Notice what Jesus turns upside down about power, greatness, and service.

Each day: 5 min pray · 10 min read · 10 min reflect · 5 min respond. Open the ESV Bible
